# Provenance for F248 V2

## Origin

F248 V1 collected exact one-row, duplicate-row, reciprocal-output, and
inverse-point bounds for scalar-lift and norm-one-torus sources. A hostile
audit accepted its mathematics. A later strict statement-only reconstruction
failed because V1's family-specific corollaries referred to constructions,
markers, orientations, and exponent grammars that were not defined in the
statement.

V2 uses the narrow repair. It removes every such specialization. It retains
only self-contained general theorems and bank bounds conditional on the
explicit parameters \(K\), \(H\), and \(H'\).

## Mathematical inputs

The V2 statement defines every nonstandard object used in its conclusions.
The proof uses these classical facts:

- CRT for two distinct odd primes;
- cyclicity of the unit group modulo an odd prime square;
- the order of split and nonsplit norm-one tori over a finite field;
- growth of positive solutions to Pell's equation;
- the standard subexponential divisor bound.

No named project proposition is a premise of V2. The exact-square gcd screen
is restated and proved. The scalar power maps, principal fibre, torus groups,
clean sets, powered image orders, and all probability spaces are defined in
the packet.

## New and retained proof content

V2 retains the following proof content from V1:

1. the full-lift square bound \(K/N\) and the two-adic criterion for the
   conditional half-factor law;
2. the exact \(4/N\) and \(2/N\) principal-fibre laws;
3. the squarefree-kernel proof of the fixed-past \(2/N\) bound;
4. the mixed-second-root equivalence;
5. the canonical duplicate and reciprocal-output divisor bounds;
6. the raw and coprime-image powered torus Pell and duplicate bounds;
7. the torus inverse identity and divisor bound.

V2 adds a single consolidated conditional bank corollary. It derives that
corollary only from the displayed general bounds and explicit asymptotic
hypotheses.

## Deliberate removals

V2 removes all exact constants, image-size lower bounds, residual-order
claims, and quasipolynomial-bank consequences tied to an external hard
family. It also removes every reference to a covered signed-power grammar.
No replacement family is asserted.

The reciprocal-output result still concerns

\[
v=[u^{-1}]_{N^2}.
\]

It does not transfer to a value obtained by canonically lifting the inverse
base modulo \(N\). The torus bounds still do not control ordinary
integer-prime parity dependencies among unrelated distinct rows.
