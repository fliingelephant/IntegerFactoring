## 4. Direct screening, including prime powers

\[

\]

be odd and let \(z^2\equiv1\bmod N\). For each odd prime \(p_i\), the two
integers \(z-1\) and \(z+1\) cannot both be divisible by \(p_i\). Since their
product is divisible by \(p_i^{\alpha_i}\), the full prime power divides
