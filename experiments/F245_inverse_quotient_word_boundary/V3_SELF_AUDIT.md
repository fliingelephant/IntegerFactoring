## Reconstruction checks

1. A raw inverse seed and a raw discriminant are uniform before their gcd
   screens.  Their accepted values are exact conditionally uniform units.
2. The inverse-quotient bank includes all singleton and pair-difference
   entries.  Its history-wise union bound is taken before adaptive word
   selection.
3. Signed-power words and exponents are materialized in binary.  A stopped
   run cannot hide an exponentially longer exponent in a short circuit.
4. Common-order accumulation is granted adversarially.  Every valid token
   divides one displayed shifted gcd, so the lcm over all orientations
   divides `12`.  Tokens have no unlisted factor exit.
5. A discriminant is screened before use.  A coefficient pair is uniform
   conditional on the complete past.  The coefficient and norm gcds exhaust
   the nonclean branch, and the inverse in Hilbert--90 normalization occurs
   only after the norm is a certified unit.
6. The exponent is fixed before its fresh torus point.  The clean branch is
   independent uniform on the two full local tori.
7. The endpoint screen, the global-return gate, every two-primary chain
   exponent, both signed-identity screens, and their exact factor rules are
   stated.  Every clean factor event implies a local return under the fixed
   endpoint exponent.
8. The exact conditional two-primary success law is included, although the
   obstruction uses only the local-return containment.
9. Direct gcds of inverse words are covered by bank incidences at `p` or
   `q`.  Every other permitted multiplicative factor is deterministically
   coprime to `N` on the marker family.
10. The stopped-run proof conditions only on the bank prefix available
    before a clean draw.  It does not condition a torus point on a future
    bank event.
11. Raw rejected draws are charged.  The cutoff bound lists the bank,
    nonclean, and clean contributions and exhausts every legal output.
12. Markov truncation applies to a fixed numerical-QP machine and a fixed
    bound before the sufficiently large family member is chosen.
