## Actual bottleneck and next action

The conditional decoding step is now explicit. For the current modular-cap
route, the missing operation is a succinct, feasible, 1.01-relative optimizer
for

\[
 S=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb Z_{>0}^2:xy\geq N,\ xy\equiv N\pmod M\},
 \qquad N/16<M\leq N/8,
\]

with uniform quasipolynomial bit cost. Per-patch support is insufficient
because there are \(2^{h-1}\) patches. The cap-line certificate reduces
constants and gives a numerical \(N^{1/3}\) tree, but it does not approach
the target exponent.

The next focused question is geometry-preserving signed-character
localization, or an equivalent operation that groups many cap-line classes
at once. Any successor must retain canonical representative placement. The
retained equal-histogram cap counterexample proves this loss for the fixed
\((1,1)\) histogram; it does not rule out the new public odd-weight menu,
whose unwrapped localization remains missing. Test a concrete recurrence or
character-sum evaluator against that counterexample before scaling. Merely
increasing degree or precision does not address this operation.

Focused reading lead: arXiv:2608.21346 studies complete shifted-product
Kloosterman sums at odd prime powers; only its abstract was read, and no
theorem or modulus-two application is assumed here.

Do not use first-factor timing as a tree-cost proxy. F288's seeded random and
explicit last-subtree audits show why traversal bias matters. The retained
F289 scaling JSON reports 16 exact and 8 budget-exhausted queries. An earlier
live run reported 15 and 9, but its full JSON is unavailable and was not
reconstructed.
