## Mathematical dependencies

The proof uses only:

1. polynomial coefficient comparison over an integral domain;
2. the rank-by-minors criterion over a field;
3. the definition of integer Smith determinantal divisors;
4. the discriminant as a product of squared root differences;
5. multiplicativity of the resultant;
6. elementary factorial-product telescoping; and
7. the elementary balanced-semiprime inequalities
   \(p\leq\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor<q\) and
   \(\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor<2p\).

No external paper, web source, unproved conjecture, hidden factor oracle,
order oracle, heuristic distribution, or numerical experiment is used.

## Exact contribution and exclusions

F273 contributes a coherent named-grammar boundary:

- arbitrary division-free polynomials in independent child coordinates
  cannot retain both child-zero orientations without the parent product
  factor;
- a single local rank defect reaches only the last Smith invariant;
- the full derivative resultant is the square of a superfactorial and is
  itself a direct factor gate;
- literal dyadic cross-resultants generate a linear offset family, while
  the elementary telescope invokes the same weighted remote product.

It does not close the general interval-product seam, rule out special
affine or finite-characteristic identities, lower-bound succinct matrix
algorithms, or factor integers.
