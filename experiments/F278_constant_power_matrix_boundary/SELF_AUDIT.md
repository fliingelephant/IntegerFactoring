# F278 author self-audit

## Verdict

`PASS` as a narrow proof-only candidate. Fresh hostile and statement-only
audits are required before promotion.

## 1. Endpoint-cost audit

The matrix is explicit. Binary powering uses `O(log B)` matrix products.
The quasipolynomial dimension is counted in both time and space. No large
implicit state is treated as free.

The result is only an evaluator theorem. It is not called a factor signal.

## 2. Jordan audit

The Jordan claim assumes `h<=d<p` and `h<=B`. These inequalities make every
`j!` a unit and place each falling-product interval below `q`.

The exact local condition is `j>s`. The same condition holds for the direct
product `B(B-1)...(B-j+1)`. The packet therefore does not call the Jordan
entry a new signal.

The preservation of a nonzero Jordan block is stated only after the direct
`gcd(B,N)` screen. Then the formal derivative `B alpha^(B-1)` is nonzero in
both local fields. A zero block dies in both fields when `h<=B`.

## 3. Semisimple audit

The collision theorem assumes invertibility and semisimplicity. The
discriminant specialization also assumes the input characteristic
polynomial is separable. Under those hypotheses, powered collision is
exactly equality of two powered eigenvalues, hence ratio torsion.

The theorem does not identify arbitrary coordinate cancellation with a
collision. That exclusion is explicit.

## 4. Quadratic audit

The condition `gcd(delta Delta,N)=1` makes both roots nonzero and distinct
in each local quadratic etale algebra. The lower-left matrix-power entry is
exactly `U_B`.

The split orientation uses the ordinary group of order `r-1`. The
irreducible orientation uses the norm-one torus of order `r+1`. Substitution
of `B=p+s` gives exponents `s+1` and `s-1`, respectively. No probability or
all-input separation claim is made.

## 5. `N`-dependence audit

The recurrence coefficients may be arbitrary public functions of `N`.
Cayley-Hamilton freezes them only with respect to the exponent index. The
packet explicitly refuses a universal recurrence lower bound. It also
refuses an impossibility theorem for higher-dimensional additive sums.

## 6. Frobenius audit

The fact that eigenvalues of `C^r` are `r`-th powers is not confused with a
public construction of the genuine local Frobenius operator on a CRT-glued
algebra. The factor-bearing status of such an operator is prior context, not
a premise in any F278 proof.

The residual factor `C^s` in `C^(p+s)` remains. The quadratic and
semisimple theorems analyze only their stated clean forms.

## 7. Search and evidence audit

No source code, generator, runner, compilation, local run, remote run,
benchmark, fixture, corpus, or empirical result belongs to F278. No durable
ledger was edited.

The no-search disposition is scoped to three named mechanisms: Jordan
coefficients, clean semisimple collisions, and clean quadratic companions.
The packet does not forbid a future search after a new exact
higher-dimensional signal law is supplied.

## 8. Deliberate nonclaims

F278 gives no all-input factoring algorithm, general matrix lower bound,
general recurrence lower bound, semilinear impossibility theorem, or
classification of arbitrary `d>=3` exponential sums.
