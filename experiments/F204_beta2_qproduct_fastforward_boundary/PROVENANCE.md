# F204 provenance

F204 starts from F203's fixed `m=2` selector

\[
 A(N)=\sum_{d\mid N}d\chi_4(d),
\]

whose exact value factors every promised balanced distinct odd semiprime.
F203 supplied the formal Lambert-series coefficient but no evaluator.

The closest prior promoted routes are:

- P29, where the untwisted level-two Eisenstein coefficient is exactly the
  ordinary divisor-sum trace and no evaluator is supplied;
- P170, where a uniform growing bank of small-modulus evaluations of that
  coefficient reconstructs the full trace and factors, while fixed-index
  character twists have rank one; and
- P175 and P177, which identify the beta-two core as selection of a hidden
  reciprocal prefix or its equivalent arithmetic-progression cell.

F204 differs materially from P29 and P170. It audits a fixed odd-character
q-product proposed as a possible remote-coefficient engine. The binary norm is
not merely observed to be weak: its full formal solution space has one free
logarithmic coefficient at every odd index. The modular and Mahler sections
then test two independent sources of an additional fast-forward equation.

The product, logarithmic derivative, formal freedom, cusp comparison, and
odd-prime root norm are proved directly. The proof uses three standard
background results:

1. `Gamma_Q(x) -> Gamma(x)` as `Q -> 1` for fixed positive `x`;
2. the finite-kernel characterization of automatic sequences; and
3. Christol's theorem: a power series over a finite field is algebraic over
   the rational-function field if and only if its coefficient sequence is
   automatic.

The Mahler reduction uses Christol only after an explicit primitive integral
clearing step and an explicit proof that the mod-two coefficient sequence has
an infinite two-kernel.

Ordinary public background was checked for q-Pochhammer asymptotics, automatic
power series, and reduction of Mahler equations. No search was made for a
factoring solution, no literature-priority claim is made, and no external
source is used as evidence for the new named-model boundary.
