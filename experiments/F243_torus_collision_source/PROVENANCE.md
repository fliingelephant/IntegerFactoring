# F243 provenance

F243 was opened after F242 supplied an exact unfactored-word Miller law for
all four quadratic-torus orientations and P206 identified distinct-integer
modular collision energy as the ordinary word source.

The new proof combines those interfaces.  Its first new observation is that
`(N^2-1)^n` is a simultaneous support baseline for every shifted order.  Its
conditional divisor theorem then applies P206 to `N^2-1` and pays F242's
constant orientation loss.

The task also asked whether quadratic-algebra objects, random
discriminants, canonical modular representatives, or Pell sequences create
aliasing unavailable to ordinary integer differences.  The exact local
counts, coordinate atom bound, gcd-divisor harmonic bound, and Pell
meta-order law answer those questions for the named sources.

The numerical work was preregistered before its official run.  F243-R1 was
invalidated by a local implementation smoke and is preserved only as a
workflow failure.  F243-R2 used disjoint inputs and ran remotely under the
frozen one-process cap.  Its finite patterns guided the scoped interpretation
but do not prove the theorem.

The closest prior results are F241/P206, F242, F237's ordinary meta-order
warning, and F170/P156 plus F172/P158 on ordinary/torus common orders.  F243
does not claim that accumulated common orders are sufficient.  It targets
unequal shifted residuals through the F242 probability law.
