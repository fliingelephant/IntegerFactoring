# F244 provenance and novelty boundary

The closest positive result is F242, which extends P205's unfactored-word
Miller law to every quadratic-torus orientation.  F244 does not alter that
law.  It supplies the public choice `W=N+J`, which makes all four orientation
exponents equal to `N^2-1`, and derives the exact joint residual identities.

P156 combines one ordinary and one Jacobi-minus-one exact common order.
F244 grants all four sign orientations and proves that their total possible
common capacity is `gcd(p^2-1,q^2-1)/2`.

P158 gives an infinite family with bounded shifted common gcds.  F244 uses a
different sequential CRT--Linnik construction.  It simultaneously embeds
one exponential selected prime in each of `p-1,p+1,q-1,q+1`, makes the
opposite hidden prime primitive at that selected prime, and fixes the four
shifted gcds exactly.  This closes the larger combination of bounded common
capacity with every numerical-quasipolynomial-bit product of signed power
words.

F237 is a finite obstruction for consecutive ordinary meta-order words.
F244 is asymptotic and covers arbitrary selected signed exponents under the
word-bit bound, but only for the stated product grammar.
