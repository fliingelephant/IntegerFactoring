# F99 — canonical power trajectories can add nullity without adding information

**Status:** exact structural obstruction. This kills one canonical power
trajectory and raw nullity growth as a progress measure. It does not refute
the full multi-seed C2T rule in F98.

## 1. Exact orbit-collapse theorem

Let \(a\ge 2\), let \(m\ge 3\), and suppose

\[
N=\frac{a^m-1}{k}
\]

is an odd integer, where \(1\le k<a\). For \(1\le r<m\), assume
\(a^r<N\) and \(a^{m-r}<N\). Put

\[
c_r=a^r,
\qquad
w_r=a^{m-r}.
\]

Then \(w_r\) is the least positive inverse of \(c_r\pmod N\), and every
canonical-inverse relation on this orbit has the same exact integer value:

\[
\boxed{c_rw_r=a^m=1+kN.}
\]

If \(m\) is odd and the square-normalized basis contains the block \(a\),
every nonzero binary relation column from this trajectory is the same
column. A dependency must use an even number \(2t\) of these columns. Its
exact positive root is

\[
R=(a^m)^t=(1+kN)^t\equiv1\pmod N.
\]

Thus every duplicate-column kernel direction has global root \(+1\):

\[
\gcd(R-1,N)=N,
\qquad
\gcd(R+1,N)=1.
\]

This remains true if one copy of the relation was already retained. Adding
exact copies enlarges the binary kernel, but the new kernel generators have
root \(+1\). They do not enlarge the image of the normalized-root map.

When \(a\) is already a prime basis block, all trajectory endpoints are
powers of that block. Exact gcd refinement cannot split an old block or add
a new block.

The proof is direct. The identity \(a^m=1+kN\) gives
\(c_rw_r\equiv1\pmod N\). The size assumptions make \(w_r\) the canonical
inverse. The remaining claims follow from the identical exact relation
values.

## 2. Trial-hard P98-stable C2T witness

Take

\[
m=17,
\qquad
N=\frac{3^{17}-1}{2}
 =64{,}570{,}081
 =1{,}871\cdot34{,}511.
\]

Both factors are prime. Their predecessor factorizations are

\[
1870=2\cdot5\cdot11\cdot17,
\qquad
34510=2\cdot5\cdot7\cdot17\cdot29.
\]

For \(1871\), the Lucas witness \(14\) satisfies
\(14^{1870}\equiv1\pmod{1871}\). For the prime divisors
\(r=2,5,11,17\) of \(1870\), the residues
\(14^{1870/r}\pmod{1871}\) are respectively

\[
1870,\ 932,\ 1838,\ 81,
\]

and the gcd of each residue minus one with \(1871\) is one.
For \(34511\), the witness \(7\) satisfies
\(7^{34510}\equiv1\pmod{34511}\). For
\(r=2,5,7,17,29\), the corresponding residues are

\[
34510,\ 30233,\ 8712,\ 26842,\ 16242,
\]

and again all five gcds are one. Lucas's primality criterion proves both
primality claims.

The input length and C2T trial bound are

\[
n=26,
\qquad
n^2=676.
\]

Both factors exceed \(676\), so the declared small-gcd screen does not find
them.

The P98 parameters are

\[
g=\gcd(1870,34510)=170,
\qquad
(A,B)=(11,203),
\]

and

\[
\gcd(11\cdot203,N-1)=1.
\]

Thus this is a trial-hard stable semiprime in the exact project sense.

The initial C2T seed \(s=3\) has

\[
3^{-1}_{\rm can}=3^{16}=43{,}046{,}721,
\qquad
3\cdot3^{16}=3^{17}=1+2N.
\]

Its square-normalized support is the single prime block \(3\), with odd
exponent \(17\). C2T's two declared words therefore reduce to the power
trajectory \([3^e]_N\) and the already processed residue \(3\).

The residue \(3\) has order \(17\) modulo each prime factor of \(N\):
\(3^{17}\equiv1\), and the prime order \(17\) leaves only orders one and
seventeen; order one is impossible. For every \(1\le r\le16\),

\[
c_r=3^r,
\qquad
w_r=3^{17-r},
\qquad
c_rw_r=3^{17}=1+2N.
\]

The range \(0\le e\le n^2\) only revisits these 17 residues. After duplicate
residues are skipped, every retained nontrivial presentation is an exact
copy of the seed relation. Nullity increases, but every new root is global
\(+1\).

The usual direct sign screens also fail. For \(1\le r\le16\),

\[
\gcd(c_r-w_r,N)=1,
\qquad
\gcd(c_r+w_r,N)=1.
\]

For the minus sign, equality modulo either prime would give
\(3^{2r}=1\), which is impossible because \(17\nmid2r\). For the plus sign,
it would put \(-1\) in the odd-order group generated by \(3\), which is
also impossible. Every endpoint is a unit and a power of the known prime
block \(3\), so this trajectory causes no endpoint gcd factor and no block
split.

This witness does **not** prove that F98 fails on \(N=64{,}570{,}081\).
Other initial seeds and other trajectories can still make progress. It
proves that one complete \(n^2\)-long C2T trajectory can satisfy the
diagnostic “nullity increased” while adding no factor-relevant information.

## 3. Private rows can persist along consecutive canonical residues

The opposite obstruction is also possible: consecutive canonical residues
do not, by themselves, force a binary dependency.

For every sufficiently large \(T\), there is a distinct odd semiprime
\(N_T\) and \(T\) consecutive power residues

\[
c_e=2^e,
\qquad
1\le e\le T,
\]

whose canonical relation values have linearly independent square classes.
The input bit length is \(n_T=\Theta(T^2)\), so this is a polynomial-length
trajectory \(T=\Theta(\sqrt{n_T})\).

Put \(K_e=2^e-1\). By Bertrand's postulate, choose distinct primes

\[
2^{T+e}<q_e<2^{T+e+1}
\qquad(1\le e\le T).
\]

Use the Chinese remainder theorem to choose a reduced residue \(a\pmod M\),
where

\[
M=2^T\prod_{e=1}^Tq_e^2,
\]

such that

\[
a\equiv1\pmod{2^T},
\qquad
a\equiv(q_e-1)K_e^{-1}\pmod{q_e^2}.
\]

Choose two different reduced residue classes modulo \(7M\) that reduce to
\(1\) and \(a\pmod M\), and whose least positive representatives exceed
\(M\). There are enough lifts to avoid divisibility by seven. Dirichlet's
theorem supplies distinct primes \(p_T,r_T\) in these classes. Linnik's
theorem lets them be chosen with

\[
p_T,r_T\le(7M)^{O(1)}.
\]

Set \(N_T=p_Tr_T\). Then \(N_T\equiv a\pmod M\), both factors exceed \(M\),
and

\[
\log N_T=\Theta(\log M)=\Theta(T^2).
\]

Because \(N_T\equiv1\pmod{2^T}\) and \(N_T>2^T\), the canonical inverse of
\(c_e=2^e\) is

\[
w_e=\frac{1+K_eN_T}{2^e}<N_T.
\]

Thus

\[
P_e=c_ew_e=1+K_eN_T.
\]

The CRT conditions give

\[
P_e\equiv q_e\pmod{q_e^2}.
\]

For \(j\ne e\), reduction modulo \(q_e\) gives

\[
P_j\equiv1-K_jK_e^{-1}
 =(K_e-K_j)K_e^{-1}
 \not\equiv0\pmod{q_e},
\]

because \(0<|K_e-K_j|<2^T<q_e\). Hence \(q_e\) occurs to odd valuation in
column \(e\) and in no other column. Each column has a private row. The
columns are linearly independent over \(\mathbb F_2\), even after complete
exact refinement.

This construction does not claim P98 stability, no block split, or failure
of direct gcd screens. Its trajectory has length
\(\Theta(\sqrt{n_T})\), not C2T's \(n_T^2\). Its exact force is narrower:
there is no general principle that a polynomial number of consecutive
canonical residues must close a binary relation. A theorem for C2T's
specific \(n^2\) menu must use more arithmetic than consecutiveness.

## 4. Consequence for the F98 progress measure

Relation count and binary nullity are not sufficient progress measures.
Before C2T counts a dependency as progress, it must quotient out exact
repeated relation values and test whether the new kernel direction enlarges
the normalized-root image. The \(m=17\) witness changes the interpretation
of a large relation batch: many columns can be new as records and dependent
as vectors while every new root candidate is exactly the old global root.

This obstruction does not apply to the current F98 certificate. F98 removes
repeated exact relation values before decoding, reports that all 166 selected
values are distinct, and exhibits a non-global root that factors its modulus.
F99 only shows that future progress claims cannot use raw relation count or
raw nullity alone.

The full F98 rule remains open because another seed can still create a new
square class, a non-global root, or a real refinement split.
