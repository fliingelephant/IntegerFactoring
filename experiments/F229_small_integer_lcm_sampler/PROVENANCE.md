   common-primary block.
2. Specialize P165's bounded-gap hostile family to one fixed even gap
   \(q-p=d\).
3. Reduce \(N-1\) modulo both predecessor exponents. The signs differ:
   \(+d\) modulo \(p-1\), and \(-d\) modulo \(q-1\).
4. Observe that a positive numerical-QP integer has \(X^d<p\), giving a
   deterministic zero-return theorem rather than only a small probability.
5. Extend the kill test to arbitrary uniform integer intervals by degree
   root counting.
6. Test whether long products escape merely by mixing. Projection of an
   exact uniform generated-subgroup element is uniform, while smooth-number
   density makes each projected subgroup \(p^{1-o(1)}\)-sized. Its fixed
   \(d\)-torsion is therefore exponentially sparse.
7. Isolate the unclosed intermediate law: a long nonuniform word must cross
   a linear log-height threshold and bias the fixed-degree carry/divisibility
   value \(X^d-1\) toward exactly one hidden field.
