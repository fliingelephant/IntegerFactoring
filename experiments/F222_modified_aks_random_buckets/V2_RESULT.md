# F222 V2 result

The modified homogeneous AKS route now has a scoped all-scale obstruction for
fresh uniform shifts.

For `r<d=q-p<p-1`, a complete raw coefficient scan has probability at most

\[
{rd\over p-1}+{rd(r+1)\over q-1}
\]

of exposing a proper coefficient gcd.  Adding the full local-nullity or
resultant event gives probability at most

\[
{2rd\over p-1}+{rd(r+3)\over q-1}.
\]

Baker--Harman--Pintz constructs an infinite balanced family with
`d=Theta(p^(3/5))`.  On it, even an adaptive numerical-QP bank of
nonstandard numerical-QP moduli, with a fresh uniform unit shift after each
modulus is fixed, succeeds with probability only `2^(-Omega(n))`.

This is integer-specific.  The proof uses the Frobenius collapse at the two
hidden primes, the actual integer gap, a cleared negative exponent, and an
unconditional prime-in-short-interval theorem.

The obstruction does not touch nonuniform integer-correlated shifts or joint
information in typical nonzero coefficients.  Those are the exact remaining
Las Vegas possibilities in this modified-AKS channel.
