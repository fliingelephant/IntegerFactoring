## Frozen material claims

1. On one fixed even-gap infinite family \(q=p+d\), every unit integer base
   satisfies
   \[
   \gcd(X^{N-1}-1,N)=\gcd(X^d-1,N).
   \]
2. Every global-return primary block from the P197 tests divides \(d\), so
   arbitrary lcm accumulation remains bounded by \(d\).
3. Every positive numerical-QP-height integer base has exactly zero return
   on all sufficiently large members of the family.
4. A product word needs total log-height at least
   \(n/(2d)+O(1)\) before any return is possible.
5. A fresh uniform integer from any history-selected initial interval has
   useful probability at most
   \((4+8d)p^{-1/d}=2^{-\Omega(n)}\).
6. Granting an exact uniform element of the whole subgroup generated by all
   rational primes through a standard QP bound still gives return
   probability \(2^{-n/2+o(n)}\). Smooth-number density, not independent
   local orders, proves the projected subgroup-size lower bound.
7. The surviving source must be a long nonuniform word law whose wrap in
   \(X^d-1\) is biased toward a factor- or new-block-producing part of the
   union of the two local \(d\)-torsion sets.
