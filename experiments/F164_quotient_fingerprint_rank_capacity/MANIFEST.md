## Exact scope

The candidate proves that public `M`-th-power fingerprints count the same
represented quotient cosets in every hidden prime-power component unless a
pairwise difference already factors `N`. A capped layer scan for one new
released block then gives a factor, a `(B+1)`-fold capacity gain, or a new
aligned integer relation. The resulting relation-rank deficit `delta` and
retained bank size `kappa` obey

\[
\kappa\ge(B+1)^\delta.
\]

If a full-rank retained relation lattice has index `D=M*kappa`, Smith normal
form gives a larger exact common cyclic subgroup and a public generator.

The candidate also gives a legal canonical-inverse CRT obstruction at
`N=341`: two distinct coprime released blocks both have local relative order
five above cap two, but the second lies in the subgroup generated by the
first and the old sign. This blocks any unconditional multiplication law
based only on block count, integer coprimality, or individual beyond-cap
certificates.
