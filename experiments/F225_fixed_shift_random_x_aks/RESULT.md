# F225 result

The fixed-shift random-point channel does not repair the large-gap branch.

For `c=2p-q>=3`, the exact local root sets reduce to polynomials of total
root capacity `O(c)`, not `O(d)`.  This gives one-trial proper-gcd
probability `O(c/p)`.

An unconditional Baker--Harman--Pintz family has

\[
c=\Theta(p^{3/5}),\qquad d=p-\Theta(p^{3/5}).
\]

Thus `d` is linear in `p`, but a numerical-QP bank of uniform evaluation
points still succeeds with probability only `2^(-Omega(n))`.

The surviving target is a genuinely integer-biased point law or a method
that extracts certified information from typical nonzero values.
