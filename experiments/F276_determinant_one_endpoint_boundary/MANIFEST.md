## Exact claims

1. Every rational full-matrix two-endpoint cocycle is a rational endpoint
   gauge.
2. Every separable additive length law is a constant matrix power. In the
   determinant-one case, the constant matrix has determinant one and the
   rational gauge has nonzero constant determinant.
3. The dimension-two rational factorial lift fails its denominator-unit
   audit exactly at the hidden factor.
4. A constant Jordan block realizes the central-binomial factor signal only
   with explicit dimension `H+1`, or with an implicit selected-entry query
   equal to the existing binomial evaluator target.
5. A polynomial unimodular moving direct-summand line can carry only a
   polynomial-ring unit multiplier.
6. Unimodular transport preserves the ideal generated by all state
   coordinates.
7. Every affine `SL_2(Z[X])` transition is a constant step times one
   nilpotent shear. After rational conjugation, its generic scalar coordinate
   is an affine-coefficient second-order continuant.
8. Holonomicity alone supplies no endpoint algorithm.
