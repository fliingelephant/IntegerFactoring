# F288 statement-only verification request

Let N>0 and let L be a translate of a lattice in R^2. Define

    S={ (x,y) in L : x>0, y>0, xy>=N }.

Claim: every vertex P=(x,y) of conv(S) is the unique minimizer over S of the
linear objective (X,Y) -> yX+xY. No integrality, rationality, full rank, or
boundedness assumption beyond the stated lattice-translate condition is
intended. Verify the claim or give a counterexample, with attention to
positive coordinates, possible ties, and unbounded sets.

Separately, for a positive integer M and P=(p,q) satisfying pq=N in one
coordinate residue coset modulo M, verify that every positive slope lambda
strictly between (q-M)/p and (q+M)/p uniquely exposes P over the positive
points of that coset with product at least N.

These are geometric statements. They assert no factoring runtime bound.
