## Exact scope

F208 proves exact relocations and obstructions only inside:

1. reciprocal-floor and Dirichlet-hyperbola transforms;
2. totalized floor reciprocity;
3. Dedekind and cotangent reciprocity;
4. triangular HNF determinant shells;
5. the literal diagonal reduced-form and principal-class route;

7. exact Archimedean magnitude extraction.

does not evaluate the signed divisor OR, supply a smaller recursive child,
give a selector algorithm, or prove a top-level factoring theorem.
