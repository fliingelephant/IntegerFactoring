# F251 provenance

F248 proved one-row, duplicate, inverse-pair, and principal-lift
fixed-past bounds. It left an unrelated nonduplicate product of two torus
rows open. The present packet addresses that boundary.

The initial concern was that the generalized Pell equation attached to an
arbitrary past product could have many inequivalent primitive seeds. The
special equation obtained from

\[
P(1+Dy^2)=R^2
\]

does not have that defect. After writing (P=au^2\), every solution element
has norm (a\) and first coefficient divisible by (a\). The quotient of any
two solution elements is therefore an integral norm-one unit. Exponential
spacing gives a count independent of (P\).

The proof uses only elementary integer valuation, conjugation in a real
quadratic algebra, and a union bound. It uses no numerical evidence and no
external theorem about class numbers or the number of generalized-Pell
orbits.
