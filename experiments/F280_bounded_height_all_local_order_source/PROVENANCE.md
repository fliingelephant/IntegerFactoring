## Exact literature composition

1. Nir Proposition 1.2 returns a factor, a prime report, or a scanned
   integer \(a\leq D^2+D\) with global order greater than \(D\).
2. The explicit scan \(\gcd(a^e-1,N)\), \(1\leq e\leq D\), returns a factor
   or certifies order greater than \(D\) modulo every rational prime
   divisor of \(N\).

Harvey--Hittmeir Theorem 1.1.
