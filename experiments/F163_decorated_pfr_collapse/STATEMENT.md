# F163 — pure decorated-PFR structure collapses to the public parity section

**Status:** proof-only candidate. This is an exact obstruction to one proposed
Babai/PFR use. It is not a factoring algorithm and not a lower bound against
PFR applied to integer presentations, carries, provenance, or another richer
source state.

## Closest prior results and the material strengthening

P35 shows that public high-dimensional relation lattices preserve the wrong
local intersection. P85 shows that a fixed word menu can miss a maximal
abstract subgroup escape. P108 identifies useful cross-layer gain with a
non-global normalized-root direction. P138 packages every retained square
relation as a decorated lift and proves the exact split-or-section law. P142
shows that completing a consistent section is decoder-inert but can change a
later integer-refinement grammar. P143 gives a deterministic
quasipolynomial sparse section source.

P138 already notes that parity rank and small doubling alone cannot force a
factor. The material strengthening here is exact and algorithmic:

> after the P138 consistency test succeeds, the parity projection is a public
> Freiman isomorphism on the whole subgroup generated by the observed
> decorated lifts. It preserves every iterated sumset, additive relation, and
> coset/subspace cover exactly.

Thus applying PFR to the decorated group, rather than to parity vectors, does
not add information on the no-factor branch. Large doubling is not a monotone
capacity either: it can occur inside the same already known root-global graph,
without increasing its span or its hidden-root kernel.

## 1. Exact extension and public graph theorem

Use the P138 hypotheses. Thus `N >= 3` is odd, the pairwise-coprime positive
unit blocks `q_1,...,q_m` have independent rational square classes, and

\[
E_Q(N)=\{(v,z):z^2\equiv Q(v)\pmod N\}
\]

has the P138 product. Let

\[
R_N=\{r:r^2\equiv1\pmod N\},
\qquad
\Delta=\{+1,-1\},
\]

and put

\[
\overline E=E_Q(N)/\Delta,
\qquad
K=R_N/\Delta.
\]

Then there is an exact sequence of binary vector spaces

\[
0\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow\overline E
\overset{\pi}{\longrightarrow}V_Q(N)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{1}
\]

Let `A` be any explicit, root-aware-deduplicated set of retained decorated
lifts in `overline E`. Put

\[
H=\langle A\rangle,
\qquad
W=\pi(H).
\]

There is an exact dichotomy.

1. If `H intersect K` is nonzero, online binary elimination reconstructs a
   non-global square root of one and factors `N`.
2. Otherwise, the restriction

   \[
   \pi|_H:H\longrightarrow W
   \tag{2}
   \]

   is a public isomorphism. Its public inverse is the P138 section

   \[
   h:W\longrightarrow H.
   \tag{3}
   \]

In the second branch, `H` is exactly the graph of `h`. For every integer
`k >= 1`, projection gives a bijection

\[
kA\longleftrightarrow k\pi(A),
\qquad
|kA|=|k\pi(A)|.
\tag{4}
\]

More generally, an additive word in elements of `A` equals another additive
word if and only if the corresponding parity words are equal. Every
Freiman-relation statistic, additive energy, affine-subspace containment,
and coset/subspace cover intrinsic to `H` therefore projects and lifts
without loss. An ambient cover can first be intersected with `H`; the theorem
makes no claim about kernel directions that are not generated by `A`.

Consequently, an algorithmic PFR routine on `A` has only two relevant
outcomes here:

- the preliminary consistency test already finds `H intersect K != 0` and
  factors `N`; or
- the PFR structure is precisely a lift, through the already public `h`, of
  structure in the parity projection `pi(A)`.

Small doubling is not needed to construct the section. P138 constructs the
full public section on `W` before PFR is called.

## 2. Large doubling is not subgroup growth

Let `W=F_2^d`, let `h:W -> overline E` be any section graph, and let
`e_1,...,e_d` be a basis. Define

\[
B_1=\{h(0),h(e_1),\ldots,h(e_d)\}.
\]

Then

\[
|B_1|=d+1,
\qquad
|B_1+B_1|=1+d+\binom d2,
\tag{5}
\]

so its doubling ratio is asymptotic to `d/2`. Nevertheless,

\[
\langle B_1\rangle=h(W)
\]

before any sumset is formed, and this subgroup has zero intersection with
`K`.

For the Hamming ball

\[
B_t=\{h(v):\operatorname{wt}(v)\le t\},
\]

one has

\[
B_s+B_t=B_{\min(s+t,d)}.
\tag{6}
\]

Repeated doubling can therefore produce genuine cardinality growth until it
fills `h(W)`, while every new point remains root-global. The generated
subgroup and its public section were already known at the first stage. If the
sumsets are listed, their size can exceed a quasipolynomial budget. If they
are represented by a basis, that compact representation is exactly the
section already returned by P138.

Thus `|A+A|` growth is not a monotone algebraic capacity for this route.

## 3. Doubling one is also root-global

For every subspace `U <= W`,

\[
A_0=h(U)
\]

satisfies

\[
A_0+A_0=A_0,
\qquad
|A_0+A_0|=|A_0|.
\tag{7}
\]

If an identity-free source set is desired, choose `u notin U` and use the
affine coset `h(u+U)`. Its sumset is `h(U)` and has the same cardinality.
PFR correctly returns exact affine-subspace structure, but that structure
lies wholly inside one root-global section graph.

## 4. Canonical-inverse section-completion realization with null direct screens

The two doubling extremes above occur in exact canonical-inverse section
records, not only in an abstract vector-space model.

Fix `d >= 1`. Choose distinct odd primes `p,q` such that

\[
p-1>4(2^d-1),
\qquad
q-1>4(2^d-1),
\]

and put `N=pq`. Let `a_p,a_q` be primitive roots. For
`j=0,...,d-1`, use the Chinese remainder theorem to choose a unit class
`c_j mod N` with

\[
c_j\equiv a_p^{2^{j+1}}\pmod p,
\qquad
c_j\equiv a_q^{2^{j+1}}\pmod q.
\tag{8}
\]

Dirichlet's theorem supplies distinct rational primes `ell_j` in these
classes. These `ell_j` are pairwise coprime nonsquare unit blocks. Let `z_j`
be the CRT root

\[
z_j\equiv a_p^{2^j}\pmod p,
\qquad
z_j\equiv a_q^{2^j}\pmod q.
\tag{9}
\]

Then `z_j^2 = ell_j mod N`. The lifts `(e_j,z_j)` generate a section
`h:F_2^d -> overline E`.

For `v in F_2^d`, let `z_v` be the section root, let
`s_v=iota_N(z_v)` be its least positive inverse, and define the exact
positive relation

\[
T_v=s_v^2\prod_j\ell_j^{v_j},
\qquad
\alpha_v=1.
\tag{10}
\]

Then `T_v = 1 mod N`, and its P138 decorated lift is exactly `h(v)` because
`alpha_v s_v^{-1}=z_v mod N`. These are the canonical-inverse section
completion records of P142. Every dependency has global normalized root.
They use the canonical inverse endpoint `s_v`; they need not equal the raw
two-endpoint product `z_v s_v` from the narrower F26 inverse-pair source.

There is also no endpoint escape. For nonzero `v`, put

\[
k(v)=\sum_jv_j2^j,
\qquad
1\le k(v)\le2^d-1.
\]

Modulo each hidden prime, the square class in (10) is the corresponding
primitive root to exponent `2k(v)`. The displayed size conditions make this
neither `+1` nor `-1`. Therefore

\[
\gcd(z_v-s_v,N)
=\gcd(Q(v)-1,N)=1,
\]

\[
\gcd(z_v+s_v,N)
=\gcd(Q(v)+1,N)=1.
\tag{11}
\]

Selecting the basis/Hamming-ball records realizes the large-doubling model.
Selecting a linear or affine subspace of completion records realizes the
doubling-one model. This construction uses the hidden factors to prove
realizability. It is not a bare-`N` source algorithm.

## 5. Exact remaining hypothesis

Pure PFR becomes useful only after a separate arithmetic statement rules out
the section graph. One sufficient target is a source operation `Phi` and two
explicit source sets whose parity spans overlap, such that on every surviving
input there is a public `u` in that overlap with

\[
h_1(u)\ne h_2(u)\quad\text{in }\overline E.
\tag{12}
\]

That disagreement is the P108/P138 non-global root and factors `N`.

Another valid target can use the richer integer state: prove that every
PFR-structured, section-consistent cell forces a strict gcd-free refinement,
a bounded-relative-order release, or another public capacity decrease, and
prove a quasipolynomial bound on all such decreases. P142, P146, and P148
show that this type of extra progress is possible, but do not force it.

The theorem does **not** rule out PFR applied to exact integer values,
canonical carries, sizes, factor-free block incidences, or provenance. Those
data are not preserved by (2). It also does not rule out a source that
already supplies incompatible decorated lifts. It rules out only the proposed
`relation columns -> decorated small/large doubling -> factor` inference when
the additive object is `overline E` alone.
