# F89 proof-blind reconstruction statement

Reconstruct or refute the claims below without reading the F89 candidate,
audit, later F89 artifacts, or later durable-state entries.

Let \(N=pq\) for distinct odd primes. Let

\[
K\le(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times
\]

be given by a public polynomial-size generator list. Put \(E=N-1\),
\(S=K^E\), and

\[
m_p=|K_p|,
\qquad
m_q=|K_q|,
\qquad
A=\frac{m_p}{\gcd(m_p,E)},
\qquad
B=\frac{m_q}{\gcd(m_q,E)}.
\]

Prove or refute that

\[
\gcd(A,B)=1
\]

and that the powered subgroup is the full product of its two local images:

\[
S=K_p^E\times K_q^E,
\qquad
|S|=AB.
\]

Check every prime-power valuation, including \(\ell=2\), unequal and equal
valuations in \(p-1,q-1\), noncyclic \(K\), and redundant generators. The
proof must not assume that \(K\) contains a graph subgroup.

Prove or refute that \(S\) contains exactly \(A+B-2\) positive separators,
with uniform density

\[
\frac1A+rac1B-rac2{AB}.
\]

If \(A,B\ge2\), verify that this density is at least
\(1/\min\{A,B\}\). Treat the cases \(A=1\), \(B=1\), and \(A=B=1\)
separately.

Prove or refute both public conditional decoders.

1. Near-uniformly sample \(S\) by powering the public generators and using
   sufficiently long independent public exponent ranges. Show that this is
   a Las Vegas polynomial-time factor extractor when \(S\ne1\) and one of
   \(A,B\) is numerically polynomial in \(\log N\). Include total-variation
   error and verified output.
2. Enumerate the subgroup generated by the powered generators, stopping
   after more than a public cap \(T\) residues. Show deterministic
   polynomial-time success when \(1<AB\le T\) and
   \(T=\operatorname{poly}(\log N)\). Check inverse-free breadth-first
   closure, wrong-input caps, deterministic lookup, and storage.

The executable procedures must not use \(p,q,m_p,m_q,A\), or \(B\).

If \(K\) contains a graph subgroup \(H\), prove or refute that
\(|H|\mid N-1\), that the power map factors abstractly through \(K/H\), and
that

\[
|S|=AB\le[K:H].
\]

Do not interpret \(K/H\) as an efficiently available quotient unless this
is proved.

Verify the fixed specializations.

1. At \(N=4033=37\cdot109\), with \(K=\langle2,5\rangle\), use the
   supplied verified local orders
   \(\operatorname{ord}_{37}(5)=36\) and
   \(\operatorname{ord}_{109}(5)=27\). Prove that
   \(5^{4032}\equiv3442\pmod{4033}\), that its local orders are one and
   three, and that
   \(\gcd(5^{4032}-1,4033)=37\). Verify \(A=1,B=3\).
2. At \(N=2047\), with \(K=\langle11,2\rangle\) and supplied exponent 22,
   prove that \(K^{2046}=1\). Explain why the unpunctured image has no
   separator and why this does not contradict the supplied punctured
   exponent 186 producing a group of order 121 with 20 separators.

State the exact scope. The theorem does not prove that feedback creates a
nontrivial image, a polynomial local image, or a polynomial full image. It
does not decode the coprime-order rectangle when both local orders are
large, handle arbitrary composites, or give an all-input factoring
algorithm.
