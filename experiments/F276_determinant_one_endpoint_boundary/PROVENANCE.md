## Search disposition

No C++ packet is proposed. Every exact positive control reaches a prior
factorial/binomial gate or fails a denominator/state-size gate. Every fully
fast-forwarded named example is a telescope, constant-power alias, or
upper-triangular sum without an exact all-input signal.

The generic affine continuant remains open, but the present theory gives it
neither a numerical-QP endpoint evaluator nor a balanced `p/q` law. A finite
coefficient scan would therefore rank small-index coincidences without
testing an executable factoring mechanism.
