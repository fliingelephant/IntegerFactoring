# Scope and source checks

The root fixed the question before external lookup: can public path-derived
square-unit rescalings increase short-attempt success after their costs are
charged? A second structural question was whether the a=-1 matrix itinerary
has a useful continued-fraction representation.

The Rust reader identified P02 and C284 as the exact hidden-root and public
rescaling interface. P52 concerns the fixed Newton map and its conjugacy to
squaring on a narrow family. P55 and P140 describe norm-one parametrizations
and the scope of the audited torus operations. None proves or refutes the
piecewise path-input feedback policy in DESIGN.md.

Focused web searches on 2026-09-07 for factoring, involutions, continued
fractions, and PPA located the following relevant primary source entries:

- [Jeřábek, Integer factoring and modular square roots](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022000015000768),
  published March 2016. The cached full text's Lemma 4.5 already gives the
  a=-1 interval involution and attributes it to Buresh-Oppenheim. The interval
  reduction is therefore not a novelty claim in this cycle.
- [Harsha, Mitropolsky, Rosen, Downward Self-Reducibility in TFNP](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ITCS.2023.67),
  published 2023-02-01. Only its publisher abstract and metadata were read.
  Its conditional complexity-class consequence is not an impossibility
  theorem and is not a dependency of this experiment.

The original Buresh-Oppenheim note was referenced at
https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~bureshop/factor.pdf, but the web reader could not
open it. No direct reading of that note is claimed. The limited searches did
not establish external novelty or a literature-wide absence of related
algorithms. No paper body, skill, or download helper was changed.
