# F90 proof-blind reconstruction statement

Reconstruct or refute the claims below without reading the F90 candidate,
audit, later F90 artifacts, or later durable-state entries.

Let \(N=pq\) for distinct odd primes. Let

\[
K\le(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times
\]

be generated by public units \(g_1,\ldots,g_m\), and put
\(\lambda=\exp(K)\).

Prove or refute the public annihilator equivalence

\[
g_i^{N-1}=1\pmod N\text{ for every }i
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\lambda\mid N-1.
\]

Assume this condition holds and that \(K\) contains a positive separator
\(x\). Choose a prime \(\ell\mid\operatorname{ord}(x)\), and put

\[
e=v_\ell(N-1),
\qquad
H=v_\ell(\lambda),
\qquad
C=v_\ell(\operatorname{ord}(x)).
\]

For

\[
j=e-C+1,
\qquad
E=(N-1)/\ell^j,
\]

prove or refute all of the following claims:

1. \(x^E\) is a positive separator of exact order \(\ell\).
2. \(K^E\) is an \(\ell\)-group of exact exponent
   \(\ell^{H-C+1}\).
3. \(K^E\) has generator rank at most two and

   \[
   |K^E|\le\ell^{2(H-C+1)}.
   \]

Check \(\ell=2\), \(C<H\), \(C=1\), \(C=H=e\), noncyclic \(K\), and
redundant generators.

Prove or refute the public deterministic algorithm. For public numerical
bounds \(L,T\), first test every generator with exponent \(N-1\). If all
tests return one, enumerate every prime \(\ell\le L\) dividing \(N-1\),
every \(1\le j\le v_\ell(N-1)\), and the public exponent
\(E=(N-1)/\ell^j\). Power all generators, enumerate their subgroup by
breadth-first multiplication, gcd-test every residue minus one, and stop a
trial after more than \(T\) distinct residues.

Show that this scan factors \(N\) if some separator \(x\) and prime
\(\ell\mid\operatorname{ord}(x)\) satisfy

\[
\ell\le L,
\qquad
\ell^{2(H-C+1)}\le T.
\]

Check completeness, the exact cap boundary, wrong-exponent work, inverse-free
closure, deterministic lookup, storage, and uniform bit complexity when the
numerical bounds, generator count, and encodings are polynomial in
\(\log N\). The executable algorithm must not use
\(p,q,\lambda,x,\operatorname{ord}(x),H\), or \(C\). The polynomial promise
must control the full exponential group-size bound, not only the raw gap
\(H-C\).

Verify the supplied specialization

\[
N=2047,
\qquad
K=\langle11,2\rangle,
\qquad
\lambda=22.
\]

Using the supplied fact that this subgroup contains positive separators of
order 11, verify the public annihilator test, the exponent
\(E=(N-1)/11=186\), the general upper bound 121, and the supplied exact
phase values \(|K^E|=121\) with 20 positive separators.

Finally, verify the claimed public structural split when combined with the
following supplied F89 theorem: if \(K^{N-1}\ne1\), that powered subgroup is
the full product of two cyclic hidden projection images of coprime orders
and hence contains a positive separator. Explain why this branch still
needs an accessibility condition. If \(K^{N-1}=1\), explain why the present
annihilator theorem still needs a supplied separator and bounded primary
image.

State the exact scope. No separator source, all-input primary bound,
arbitrary-composite reduction, or complete factoring algorithm is claimed.
