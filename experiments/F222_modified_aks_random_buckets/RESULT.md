# F222 result

The modified homogeneous AKS error has two sharply different random
channels.

1. A random shift turns one fixed local polynomial into a random cyclotomic
   twist. The exact nullity probability is an image-size count under the
   `r`-th power map.
2. On an infinite fixed-bounded-gap family, any QP bank of fresh uniform
   shifts and arbitrary nonstandard QP-size moduli has exponentially small
   probability of either a proper resultant gcd or any local annihilation.
3. Raw coefficients are not covered by that kill. When `r>2(q-p)`, their
   complete scan either factors or leaves identical local supports of size
   at most `2(q-p)`.
4. This coefficient theorem activates only when the prime gap is numerical
   QP, where Fermat already factors in QP time. The hard `q-p>=r/2` branch
   remains open.

The positive target is therefore narrower: use an integer-biased shift or a
joint multi-shift minor on the large-gap branch, and prove an inverse-QP
event law. Uniform random shifts and local annihilation are closed in the
stated model.
