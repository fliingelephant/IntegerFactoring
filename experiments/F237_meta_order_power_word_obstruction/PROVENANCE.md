# F237 provenance

The instance was selected after exploratory Sage searches over balanced
prime pairs generated by the exact modular-hyperbola condition

\[
q\equiv p^{-1}\pmod {2^m}.
\]

The search was not preregistered as a statistical experiment.  F237 makes
no frequency, distribution, or asymptotic claim, so post-selection does not
affect its existential statement.  Every retained assertion is checked by
the frozen deterministic certificate.

The closest promoted result is P204.  P204 proves that the word
`W_K=product_(k<=K)(N^k-1)` works if one residual meta-order is small.  F237
gives one exact valid input on which both whole-residual meta-orders are
large and, more strongly, one large primary component on each side is
untouched through `K=2^57`.

F237 does not claim that these instances form an infinite family.  It does
not refute the all-input source statement left open by P204.
