# F51 canonical high-digit metric kill

**Approach-family:** F12 exponent-\(N\) Teichmüller and principal-digit
carriers.

**Status:** corrected candidate narrow obstruction. A first hostile audit
confirmed every displayed formula and all five computations, but rejected an
overbroad inference from Fourier energy and raw empirical-mean error. The exact
symbolic small-bias channel is now left open below, and that failed audit remains
preserved. No proof-blind reconstruction has run. The finite runs are discovery
and certificate evidence only.

## Result

No factoring candidate survives the tested statistical channels.

Let \(N=pq\), where \(p<q<2p\) are distinct odd primes. For a uniform public
unit \(a\bmod N\), define

\[
A(a)=a^N\bmod N^2=x(a)+Nh(a),\qquad
\lambda(a)=h(a)x(a)^{-1}\pmod N,
\]

using canonical representatives \(0\le x,h,\lambda<N\).

The source has an exact local Fermat-quotient law. It implies:

1. Every point of the \(h\)-law and the \(\lambda\)-law has probability at
   most \(1/(p-1)\).
2. A set of \(L\) named output values has mass at most \(L/(p-1)\).
3. Exact output collisions, direct gcd events, and all pairwise
   local-collision gcds among polynomially many samples have exponentially
   small probability in \(\log N\).
4. A hidden-prime residue band of relative width \(\epsilon\) has mass
   \(O(\epsilon+1/p)\). At the ordinary-LLL precision
   \(\epsilon=2^{-(1+\eta)\sqrt n}\), even a recognizer for the hidden band
   would need \(2^{\Omega(\sqrt n)}\) raw samples to collect the promised
   batch. At inverse-polynomial width, P51's ordinary-LLL certificate is
   noninformative.
5. The canonical real digit has exact reflection
   \(h(N-x)=N-1-h(x)\). Its mean is the public value \((N-1)/2\), every
   centered antisymmetric statistic has mean zero, and the central
   half-interval bias is at most \(1/(2(p-1))+1/(2N)\).
6. The total Fourier energy can be large enough to forbid a pointwise
   pseudorandomness claim, but a uniform public unit frequency has an
   inverse-polynomial-sized true coefficient with only exponentially small
   probability. A local-isolating frequency is a multiple of \(p\) or \(q\),
   and its gcd already factors \(N\).

This is not a distributional or factoring lower bound. In particular, the
finite scans show that the full laws can have constant total variation from
uniform. Deterministic coarse intervals, deterministic or adaptively
recovered heavy gcd-free frequencies, nonlinear processing of the joint
\((x,h)\) graph, and engineered nonuniform bases remain open.

## 1. Closest prior route and material difference

The closest prior route is X16/P22. It bounds direct factor divisibility of
fixed canonical high digits. The present route is materially different: it
studies the distribution of nonzero \(h\) and \(\lambda\), their exact local
laws, real interval statistics, collisions, hidden residue bands, and
Fourier energy.

X50/P56 closes multiplicative linear-cocycle inconsistency. It does not
close one-point metric statistics. X43/P49 supplies the comparison class for
ordinary Fourier and histogram statistics. X45/P51 supplies the exact
ordinary-LLL boundary once an approximate-multiple source is granted.

## 2. Uniform source and simultaneous Teichmüller section

On

\[
G_N\simeq C_{p-1}\times C_{q-1},
\]

the map \(a\mapsto x=a^N\bmod N\) is an automorphism. Indeed, \(q\) is
larger than both \(p-1\) and \(q-1\). The only possible way for \(p\) to
divide \(q-1<2p-1\) is \(q-1=p\), but then the two odd primes would differ
by one. Hence

\[
\gcd\!\left(N,\operatorname{lcm}(p-1,q-1)\right)=1.
\]

Thus \(x\) is uniform in \(G_N\).

The value \(A(a)\) is the simultaneous Teichmüller lift of \(x\):

\[
A(a)\equiv[x\bmod p]_p\pmod {p^2},\qquad
A(a)\equiv[x\bmod q]_q\pmod {q^2}.                 \tag{2.1}
\]

For example,
\([a\bmod p]_p^q\) reduces to \(a^q=x\bmod p\), so uniqueness of the local
Teichmüller lift proves the first identity. The second is symmetric.
Consequently the law can equivalently be generated by uniform \(x\in G_N\)
and its unique simultaneous Teichmüller lift \(T_N(x)\).

One sample costs polynomial time without the factors: rejection-sample
\(a\bmod N\), screen it with a gcd, and compute \(a^N\bmod N^2\) by binary
exponentiation. With schoolbook multiplication this costs \(O(n^3)\) bit
operations, where \(n=\lceil\log_2(N+1)\rceil\).

## 3. Exact local Fermat-quotient law

For a prime \(r\nmid x\), define the Fermat quotient

\[
Q_r(x)=\frac{x^{r-1}-1}{r}\pmod r.                 \tag{3.1}
\]

Since \(h\equiv x\lambda\pmod N\),

\[
T_N(x)\equiv x(1+N\lambda)\pmod {N^2}.             \tag{3.2}
\]

Modulo \(p^2\), the left side is Teichmüller, so its \((p-1)\)-st power is
one. Expanding (3.2) gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
1
&\equiv x^{p-1}(1+pq\lambda)^{p-1}\\
&\equiv
(1+pQ_p(x))(1+pq(p-1)\lambda)\\
&\equiv1+p(Q_p(x)-q\lambda)\pmod {p^2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{q\lambda\equiv Q_p(x)\pmod p,\qquad
p\lambda\equiv Q_q(x)\pmod q.}                    \tag{3.3}
\]

Multiplication by \(x\) gives

\[
\boxed{qh\equiv xQ_p(x)\pmod p,\qquad
ph\equiv xQ_q(x)\pmod q.}                         \tag{3.4}
\]

There is also a carry form useful for counting. Put

\[
d_r(u)=\frac{u^r-u}{r}\pmod r,\qquad 1\le u<r.
\]

Write \(x=u+pk\), where \(1\le u<p\), \(0\le k<q\), and omit the unique
\(k\) for which \(q\mid u+pk\). Expanding the Fermat quotient after the
integer lift gives

\[
\boxed{
qh\equiv d_p(u)-k,\qquad
qu\lambda\equiv d_p(u)-k\pmod p.}                 \tag{3.5}
\]

Symmetrically, write \(x=v+q\ell\), where \(1\le v<q\),
\(0\le\ell<p\), and omit the unique \(\ell\) for which
\(p\mid v+q\ell\). Then

\[
\boxed{
ph\equiv d_q(v)-\ell,\qquad
pv\lambda\equiv d_q(v)-\ell\pmod q.}              \tag{3.6}
\]

Equations (3.3)--(3.6) are exact source laws. They also show that the local
statistics are restricted Fermat-quotient statistics, not independent
random digits.

## 4. Anti-concentration and exact collision obstruction

Fix one residue \(c\bmod p\). For each \(u\), either equation in (3.5)
restricts \(k\) to one class modulo \(p\). Since \(0\le k<q<2p\), at most
two values of \(k\) work. There are \(p-1\) choices of \(u\). Hence, for
\(Z=h\) or \(Z=\lambda\),

\[
\Pr(Z\equiv c\pmod p)\le\frac{2}{q-1}.            \tag{4.1}
\]

For a fixed residue \(c\bmod q\), equation (3.6) gives at most one
\(\ell\in[0,p)\) for each \(v\). Therefore

\[
\Pr(Z\equiv c\pmod q)\le\frac{1}{p-1}.            \tag{4.2}
\]

A fixed full value \(z\bmod N\) in particular fixes its residue modulo
\(q\), so

\[
\boxed{\max_z\Pr(Z=z)\le\frac1{p-1}.}             \tag{4.3}
\]

Consequences:

\[
\Pr(Z\in S)\le\frac{|S|}{p-1}                    \tag{4.4}
\]

for every named set \(S\subseteq\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z\), and for two
independent samples

\[
\Pr(Z_1=Z_2)
=\sum_z\Pr(Z=z)^2
\le\frac1{p-1}.                                  \tag{4.5}
\]

Likewise,

\[
\Pr(Z_1\equiv Z_2\pmod p)\le\frac2{q-1},\qquad
\Pr(Z_1\equiv Z_2\pmod q)\le\frac1{p-1}.          \tag{4.6}
\]

Thus the union bound for all pairwise gcds of differences among \(m\)
samples is

\[
\Pr(\text{some pair has a local collision})
\le {m\choose2}\left(\frac2{q-1}+\frac1{p-1}\right). \tag{4.7}
\]

When \(|S|=\operatorname{poly}(n)\) and
\(m=\operatorname{poly}(n)\), the corresponding bounds in (4.4), (4.5),
and (4.7) are \(2^{-\Omega(n)}\). This closes:

- polynomial menus of exact values;
- global intervals containing only \(\operatorname{poly}(n)\) residues;
- birthday or repeated-value statistics;
- every all-pairs gcd-of-differences search on \(h\) or \(\lambda\).

Since multiplication by the unit \(x\) preserves gcd with \(N\),
\(\gcd(h,N)=\gcd(\lambda,N)\). Equations (4.1)--(4.2) recover the direct
bound

\[
\Pr(1<\gcd(h,N)<N)
\le\frac2{q-1}+\frac1{p-1},                       \tag{4.8}
\]

consistent with P22.

## 5. Hidden residue bands and the P51 decoder boundary

Let \(R_p\subseteq\mathbb F_p\) and \(R_q\subseteq\mathbb F_q\). From
(4.1)--(4.2),

\[
\Pr(Z\bmod p\in R_p)\le\frac{2|R_p|}{q-1},\qquad
\Pr(Z\bmod q\in R_q)\le\frac{|R_q|}{p-1}.         \tag{5.1}
\]

In particular, the centered band of residues of absolute size at most
\(B<p/2\) modulo \(p\) has at most \(2B+1\) residues and mass

\[
\Pr(\|Z/p\|_{\mathbb R/\mathbb Z}\le B/p)
\le\frac{2(2B+1)}{q-1}.                           \tag{5.2}
\]

The symmetric \(q\)-bound is \((2B+1)/(p-1)\). For fixed balance these are
\(O(B/p+1/p)\): the source does not amplify a thin approximate-multiple
band.

P51's ordinary-LLL decoder needs relative error

\[
\epsilon=B/p\le2^{-(1+\eta)\sqrt n}
\]

and \(m=\Theta(\sqrt n)\) promised inliers. Even granting a recognizer for
the hidden \(p\)-band, (5.2) makes the expected raw sample requirement
\(2^{\Omega(\sqrt n)}\). At \(\epsilon=n^{-c}\), the bound permits
inverse-polynomial band mass, but P51 proves that its ordinary worst-case
LLL certificate is noninformative for every lattice dimension.

This does not rule out another decoder, a favorable error law, partial
inlier recovery, or a factor-free method that recognizes the hidden band.

## 6. Exact canonical real symmetry

The simultaneous Teichmüller section is odd:

\[
T_N(N-x)\equiv-T_N(x)\pmod {N^2}.
\]

Both sides are nonzero, so canonical representatives satisfy

\[
N^2-(x+Nh)=(N-x)+N(N-1-h).
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{h(N-x)=N-1-h(x).}                          \tag{6.1}
\]

The involution \(x\mapsto N-x\) has no fixed unit because \(N\) is odd.
Every pair has digit sum \(N-1\), and hence

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E h=\frac{N-1}{2}.}                \tag{6.2}
\]

More generally, if
\(f(N-1-z)=-f(z)\), then

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E f(h)=0.}                         \tag{6.3}
\]

This includes all odd centered moments. It makes the most immediate
canonical real statistic exactly public and factor-free.

Let \(c=\Pr(h=(N-1)/2)\). By (4.3), \(c\le1/(p-1)\). Reflection gives

\[
\Pr\!\left(h<\frac N2\right)=\frac{1+c}{2}.
\]

The uniform-grid mass is \((N+1)/(2N)\), so

\[
\left|
\Pr\!\left(h<\frac N2\right)-\frac{N+1}{2N}
\right|
\le\frac1{2(p-1)}+\frac1{2N}.                     \tag{6.4}
\]

Thus the exact central half-interval bias is exponentially small in the
input length.

Equation (6.1) does not bound arbitrary shifted or nonsymmetric intervals.
That limitation is essential.

## 7. Fourier energy and hidden-factor frequencies

For \(Z=h\) or \(Z=\lambda\), let

\[
\widehat\mu_Z(k)=\mathbb E\,e_N(kZ).
\]

Parseval and (4.3) give

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{k\bmod N}|\widehat\mu_Z(k)|^2
=N\sum_z\Pr(Z=z)^2
\le\frac{N}{p-1}.}                               \tag{7.1}
\]

For any \(\delta>0\), the number of frequencies with
\(|\widehat\mu_Z(k)|\ge\delta\) is at most

\[
\frac{N}{(p-1)\delta^2}.                          \tag{7.2}
\]

If \(K\) is uniform in \(G_N\), it can be sampled without factoring by
gcd rejection. Since \(N/\varphi(N)<2\),

\[
\boxed{
\Pr\bigl(|\widehat\mu_Z(K)|\ge\delta\bigr)
\le\frac{2}{(p-1)\delta^2}.}                      \tag{7.3}
\]

A polynomial list of independently random public unit frequencies therefore
misses every inverse-polynomial-heavy coefficient with probability
\(1-2^{-\Omega(n)}\).

The frequencies that directly isolate the \(p\)-marginal are

\[
e_p(rZ)=e_N(qrZ).
\]

Every nonzero such global frequency \(qr\), \(1\le r<p\), has
\(\gcd(qr,N)=q\). The \(q\)-local frequencies are multiples of \(p\).
Thus an explicitly supplied local-isolating frequency already displays a
factor.

For a fixed character sample \(X=e_N(kZ)\) with mean \(\theta\), the raw
empirical mean obeys the exact identity

\[
\mathbb E|\overline X_m-\theta|^2
=\frac{1-|\theta|^2}{m}.                          \tag{7.4}
\]

Equations (7.1)--(7.3) show that polynomially many independently uniform unit
frequencies have exponentially small probability of finding an
inverse-polynomial-heavy coefficient. Equation (7.4) is the exact mean-square
error of the raw empirical mean. These statements do not rule out deterministic
exceptional frequencies, adaptive frequency recovery, other estimators, exact
symbolic evaluation or amplification of exponentially small coefficients, or a
Fourier-dense nonlinear statistic.

## 8. Finite kill tests

All finite runs used named source, a 180-second hard timeout, retained logs,
and retained JSON outputs.

- F51-D01 scanned twelve balanced pairs through
  \((p,q)=(211,223)\). Point fibres stayed small. Direct gcd probability
  decreased to \(0.009202\). The full total variation from uniform remained
  about \(0.37\), which is why this artifact makes no total-variation
  pseudorandomness claim.
- F51-D02 verified (3.5)--(3.6) on the same pairs and computed exact local
  histograms. On \((211,223)\), the largest nonzero local Fourier
  magnitudes were below \(0.00845\) for \(h\) and below \(0.00281\) for
  \(\lambda\).
- F51-D03 verified (6.1) and the exact mean on all twelve pairs. It inspected
  normalized variance and five fixed thresholds; those data prove no
  asymptotic bound.
- F51-D04 scanned all 233 balanced odd-prime pairs with \(p\le101\). The
  largest observed \((p-1)D^*\) was \(1.683\) for \(h\) and \(1.910\) for
  \(\lambda\). This suggests a possible \(O(1/p)\) interval discrepancy but
  is not a proof.
- F51-D05 computed the complete finite DFT by gcd category. On
  \((211,223)\), the largest gcd-free coefficient was \(0.04048\) for
  \(h\) and \(0.04986\) for \(\lambda\). These are much larger than the
  local marginal coefficients, but finite values do not establish an
  inverse-polynomial asymptotic signal.

The run details and exact invocations are in RUN_MANIFEST.md.

## 9. Sharp scope and exact reopen condition

The proved obstruction is narrow. It closes only:

- exact values, polynomial-width named sets, and repeated-value statistics;
- direct and pairwise-collision gcd extraction;
- thin hidden residue bands at the P51 ordinary-LLL precision;
- the mean, centered antisymmetric statistics, and the central half interval
  of the canonical real digit;
- independently uniform public frequencies searched for
  inverse-polynomial-heavy coefficients by raw empirical means, and explicitly
  supplied local-isolating frequencies.

A retry is materially new only if it supplies at least one of the following:

1. A factor-free, polynomial-time recognizable coarse event or interval with
   a proved inverse-polynomial factor-dependent bias and an explicit
   extraction rule.
2. A deterministic gcd-free frequency, or a polynomial-time implicit
   heavy-frequency recovery algorithm, with a proved inverse-polynomial
   coefficient on every required input family and a factoring reduction.
3. A nonlinear joint decoder for \((x,h)\) or \((x,\lambda)\) that is not a
   multiplicative linear-cocycle syndrome and whose success is proved.
4. A source that produces P51-grade all-inlier approximate multiples in
   polynomial expected time, or a fully dimensioned decoder at
   inverse-polynomial relative error.
5. An engineered, adaptive, correlated, or nonuniform base distribution
   with a proved inverse-polynomial all-input separation law.
6. An exact symbolic evaluation or amplification of small Fourier coefficients
   or interval biases, with polynomial bit cost and a proved extraction rule.

In particular, proving a uniform discrepancy bound for all shifted intervals
would be materially new. So would a theorem that every inverse-polynomial
heavy gcd-free Fourier coefficient can be found, or that none exists. The
present point-mass and energy bounds prove neither statement.
