## New proof seam

F282 keeps the same normalized monomial difference but permits a uniform
public shift \(a\). The complete-homogeneous divided-difference identity
shows:

- the \(q\)-component vanishes for every shift; and
- the \(p\)-component is a nonzero polynomial of degree \(s+1\) in the
  shift.

The field root bound replaces the proposed deterministic two-scalar
dispatcher by a constant-success random shift. It therefore makes the F281
simultaneous-saturation conjecture unnecessary for this conditional route.
It does not resolve that conjecture and does not validate the F281 runner.

The remote-coefficient problem survives in shifted form: one now needs a
uniform numerical-QP evaluator for the normalized scalar from public
\((N,a)\). The raw difference is identically zero modulo \(N\) on the
unresolved branch, so an evaluator for the raw numerator does not suffice.
F282 supplies the probability theorem around the exact normalized interface,
not the interface implementation.
