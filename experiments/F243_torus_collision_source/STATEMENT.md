# F243 candidate — universal shifted-support stripping and torus collision boundaries

## Status and scope

This is a proof-level conditional bridge for F242 plus scoped source
obstructions.  It is not an all-input factoring algorithm.

The positive result has two layers.

1. One public word strips all prime support shared by any ordinary or torus
   shifted order.
2. If a factored `N^2-1` supplies inverse-QP uniform-divisor collision
   energy on one remaining shifted residual, F242 factors with only a
   constant orientation loss.

The negative results show that raw norm collisions, locally uniform
Hilbert--90 collisions, random discriminants, canonical torus-coordinate
carries, a factor-free gcd-divisor law, and bounded Pell windows do not by
themselves supply the missing inverse-QP law at a large residual prime.
They do not cover arbitrary nonlinear combinations or all integer carries.

## 1. One baseline for all four orientations

Let

\[
N=pq
\]

for distinct odd primes and put

\[
n=\lceil\log _2(N+1)\rceil,
\qquad A=N^2-1.
\]

For a sign pair `epsilon=(epsilon_p,epsilon_q)`, define

\[
m_p=p-\epsilon_p,\qquad m_q=q-\epsilon_q,
\qquad d_\epsilon=\gcd(m_p,m_q),
\]

\[
s_{p,\epsilon}=m_p/d_\epsilon,
\qquad s_{q,\epsilon}=m_q/d_\epsilon.
\]

Let `s_i^ext` be the largest divisor of `s_i` supported on rational primes
that do not divide `A`.  The single public word

\[
\boxed{W_0=A^n}
\]

has `O(n^2)` bits and leaves exactly the F242 residuals

\[
\boxed{r_{i,\epsilon}=s_{i,\epsilon}^{\rm ext}}
\]

for every side and every sign pair at once.

There is an exact support interpretation.  If `ell` is odd and
`ell | p-epsilon_p`, then

\[
\boxed{
\ell\mid N^2-1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\ell\mid q-\delta\text{ for some }\delta\in\{+1,-1\}.
}
\]

Thus every surviving exterior prime on the `p` side is absent from both
`q-1` and `q+1`, and conversely.  The baseline removes all cross-sign
support.  It does not bound the exterior parts.

## 2. Collision energy gives a conditional F242 source

Let `Z,Z'` be independent samples from any public exact integer law.  Put

\[
\Delta=|Z-Z'|\quad\text{when }Z\ne Z',
\qquad \Delta=1\quad\text{when }Z=Z'.
\]

For squarefree `m`, define

\[
\kappa_m=\Pr(Z\ne Z'\text{ and }Z\equiv Z'\pmod m).
\]

The P206 collision-energy identity applies unchanged to each shifted
exterior residual.  In particular, one collision modulo `ell`, followed by
the exponent `n`, supplies the full `ell`-primary part.

There is a concrete conditional theorem.  Grant a complete factorization

\[
A=N^2-1=\prod_j b_j^{a_j},
\qquad T=\tau(A),
\]

and sample `Z` uniformly from the divisors of `A`.  For every exterior
prime `ell`, let `h_ell` be the subgroup size generated by the reductions
of the `b_j` in `F_ell^*`.  Then

\[
\kappa_\ell\ge {1\over h_\ell}-{1\over T}.
\]

More generally, suppose that for every rational prime in one fixed
`s_i^ext`,

\[
\kappa_\ell\ge {1\over Q(n)}.
\]

With

\[
R=\lceil Q(n)\log(2n)\rceil,
\qquad
W=A^n\prod_{t=1}^R\Delta_t^n,
\]

the word absorbs that full shifted residual with probability at least
`1/2`.  A fresh uniform discriminant has the required hidden sign pair with
probability `1/4`.  Conditional on that orientation, F242 factors with
probability at least `1/2`.  Therefore the complete bank factors with
probability at least

\[
\boxed{1/16.}
\]

The word is numerical-QP when `Q` is numerical-QP.  The result remains
conditional because it grants the factorization of `N^2-1` and does not
prove the collision hypothesis.  The sufficient conditions
`h_ell<=Q/2` and `T>=Q` imply the displayed hypothesis after a harmless
rescaling of `Q`.

## 3. Exact split and nonsplit local laws

Fix an odd prime `ell`, a unit `D mod ell`, and

\[
\epsilon=(D/\ell),\qquad m=\ell-\epsilon.
\]

For `Q_D(a,b)=a^2-Db^2` and independent uniform vectors in
`F_ell^2`,

\[
\Pr(Q_D(V)=Q_D(V'))
={\ell^2+\ell-1\over\ell^3}.
\]

After removing the same-vector atom `1/ell^2`, the distinct-vector rate is

\[
{\ell^2-1\over\ell^3}.
\]

For the norm of a difference, the split and nonsplit cases separate:

\[
\Pr(Q_D(V-V')=0)=
\begin{cases}
(2\ell-1)/\ell^2,&\epsilon=+1,\\
1/\ell^2,&\epsilon=-1.
\end{cases}
\]

The same-vector atom is `1/ell^2`.  Thus a nonsplit norm-difference has no
distinct local zero at all, while the split rate is still only `Theta(1/ell)`.

For a uniform algebra unit `z`, Hilbert--90 normalization

\[
U=z/\bar z
\]

is uniform on the norm-one torus of order `m`.  Its trace has total
collision probability

\[
{2m-2\over m^2},
\]

and distinct-point collision probability

\[
\boxed{{m-2\over m^2}.}
\]

Writing `z=a+bw` and `z'=c+ew`, one has

\[
U=U'\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad bc-ae=0,
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{tr}(U)=\operatorname{tr}(U')
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(bc-ae)(bc+ae)=0.
\]

The same-discriminant resultants lose `D`; changing the hidden orientation
does not create a larger collision scale.  With two discriminants `D,E`,
the trace resultant is, up to a public unit,

\[
Db^2c^2-Ea^2e^2.
\]

If `D,E` are uniform in `F_ell^*` and the displayed coefficients are units,
its zero probability is exactly `1/(ell-1)`.  A nonuniform discriminant law
can improve this only through its own multiplicative correlation.

All these are local-object laws.  An integer word must also replace an exact
integer zero by one.  That subtraction can only reduce useful collision
energy and can be larger than the same-local-object atom.

## 4. Random discriminants and canonical coordinate carries are generic-scale

Take a fixed public unit discriminant `D` and the exact F242 sampler.  Its
accepted output is uniform on the product of the two hidden local tori, of
size

\[
M=(p-\epsilon_p)(q-\epsilon_q).
\]

Represent either coefficient canonically in `{0,...,N-1}`.  Every fixed
integer coefficient value has at most four torus preimages.  Therefore, for
every exterior prime `ell`, the total modular collision probability of one
canonical coefficient is at most

\[
\boxed{
{4\lceil N/\ell\rceil\over M}
\le {15\over2\ell}+{15\over2N}
< {15\over\ell}.
}
\]

Removing exact integer equality only decreases this value.  Taking the
union of both coordinate-difference words is below `30/ell`.  Thus the
canonical representative carry does not rescue either coordinate at a
super-QP exterior prime.

Similarly, a uniform unit discriminant conditioned on either Jacobi sign
has atom size `2/phi(N)`.  Its distinct-integer modular collision probability
is less than `15/(2ell)`.  Raw random discriminants also remain at the
generic scale.

These bounds do not cover cross-coordinate determinants of canonical torus
points or arbitrary nonlinear carry words.

## 5. A factor-free divisor law has a universal harmonic obstruction

The law

\[
X\text{ uniform modulo }A,
\qquad Z=\gcd(X,A)
\]

is exactly samplable without factoring `A`.  For `d|A`,

\[
\Pr(Z=d)={\varphi(A/d)\over A}\le {1\over d}.
\]

For every prime `ell` not dividing `A`, its distinct-integer collision
energy satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\kappa_\ell
\le {2(1+\log A)^2\over\ell}.
}
\]

Hence this factor-free replacement for uniform divisor sampling is
exponentially weak at an exponentially large exterior prime.  It can also
have exactly zero collision energy.  For example, if its divisor support is
injective modulo `ell`, every modular equality is an exact integer equality
and is discarded.

## 6. Pell traces reduce exactly to a new meta-order

In the public algebra

\[
\mathbb Z[w]/(w^2-(N^2-1)),
\qquad \eta=N+w,
\]

write

\[
\eta^k=X_k+Y_kw.
\]

For an exterior prime `ell`, put

\[
h_\ell=\operatorname{ord}_\ell(\eta)
\mid \ell-left({N^2-1\over\ell}\right).
\]

For exponents `I,J` uniform in `{0,...,T-1}`, the coefficient-pair
collision law is

\[
T=q h_\ell+r,
\qquad
\boxed{
\Pr(I\ne J,\ \eta^I=\eta^J)
={h_\ell q(q-1)+2rq\over T^2}.
}
\]

The trace values are `X_k=T_k(N)` and are strictly increasing as integers.
Modulo `ell`,

\[
X_i=X_j
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
i\equiv j\text{ or }i\equiv-j\pmod {h_\ell}.
\]

Consequently a `T`-term trace window has exactly zero distinct collision
when

\[
h_\ell\ge2T-2.
\]

When `h_ell` is small, the deterministic public word
`X_{h_ell}-1` already contains `ell`; a product over all QP-small indices is
QP-bit.  Random trace collisions therefore do not bypass the missing
small-meta-order theorem.

## Exact remaining gap

After the universal baseline, prove inverse-QP distinct-integer aliasing on
one shifted exterior residual without factoring `N^2-1`, or find a
cross-coordinate/nonlinear integer carry that escapes the generic and
meta-order boundaries above.
